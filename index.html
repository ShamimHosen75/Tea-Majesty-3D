Our Craft

From misted slope
to royal cup.

Four unhurried acts, perfected over generations. Scroll the journey.

I

Pluck at Dawn

Skilled hands select just two leaves and a bud while dew still clings, before the sun steals their sweetness.

05:30 AM · 1,450 m
II

Wither in Mountain Air

Leaves rest on breezy racks for up to 18 hours, softening and concentrating their aromatic oils.

18 hrs · Natural airflow
III

Roll & Oxidise

Gently rolled to release their juices, then oxidised to a burnished copper, where malt and honey notes are born.

Copper hue · Malty depth
